The officiant behind every ceremony

Hello, I'm Rosy.

My story

I believe in ceremonies
that feel truly yours.

I've always been drawn to the power of words. After years of studying literature and communication, I discovered my true calling: crafting the ceremony that ties the most important day of your life to who you truly are.

Since 2015, I've had the extraordinary privilege of officiating over 180 ceremonies across Tuscany and beyond. Each one is completely unique — because you are.

Whether intimate elopement in the Val d'Orcia or a grand celebration at a Chianti villa, I'm here to make it real and unforgettable.

See recent celebrations →
01

Deeply personal

Every ceremony begins with a real conversation. I want to know your story, your quirks, the little moments that make your love uniquely yours.

02

Expertly crafted

With a background in literature and storytelling, I write ceremonies that are emotionally resonant, beautifully structured, and truly unforgettable.

03

Fully bilingual

I officiate in both English and Italian, and can accommodate multilingual ceremonies for international couples and their families.
